The Radical Life Philosophy

A Manifesto on Human Nature, Autonomy, and the Art of Becoming

Human nature is complex.

We evolved between doubt and doubt. Mental inquisitions made it difficult to fall toward where we wished our destiny lay. And yet, what is man? A kind of idiot who debases himself with the most elementary questions of knowledge. And yet, what can we extract from this knowledge that might spark a better life in all of us?

Human beings can become anything.

Then, autonomy presented itself as a method of expression.

Do not contemplate this with the intellect. You must feel it. A connection that covers your entire life and sends you to the end on a correct path, because it is the only one that could serve your purposes.

Christopher Hyatt's phrases against conditioning are based on a simple truth: we live deceived by everything and everyone. We never fall into our true nature, free from the inquisition of the mind, awakening to who we really are, without need for approval or anything that external people offer us.

· · ·

Beyond boredom. Beyond suffering. Beyond this and that.

Let us call this philosophy: The Radical Life Philosophy.

A radical life is, consequently, a life that is serious and straight. In our antidemocratic and absolutely crude labors, we live as we please, liberated from guilt and fear. In that pragmatic style, we are the species of freedom. A species of excellent life until the end.

Committed to the life we want. Committed to the mind we want. Committed to what we want to become.

Without limits.

Until we adopt all the styles of what we desire, and can die one day well.

But to see what the essence truly is: it is to be without forms and without limits.

Only then do you reveal yourself to the true nature of existence itself.

· · ·

I hope this serves you in some way.

It is a little chaotic, and I accept that. But I hope you do not understand it with your mind. I hope you feel it.

If this moved you in some way, if some spark of non-intellectual comprehension ignited within you, then feel it. This is a finger pointing at the moon. It is not the end of the path.

This can improve your life in all aspects.

I hope it serves you.

Farewell.
