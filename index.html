Radical Logical
Perceptualism

mind without limits, resting on two points · total experience of life and the human being until the very end

Let us begin with two assertions: the world consists of experimentation, and of the mind, which is the method of perception we possess. Even when you are not using your mind, the unconscious mind is still regulating processes that interact totally and absolutely with your body. The mind is always necessary in the algorithmic, interactive, and extraordinarily creative mode of comprehension that we human beings hold. Through it, we innovate toward what is called consciousness, a product that many living beings lack because they never reached that peak of evolution.

The two points on which I believe this rests are simple: life is composed of action and, as I have just stated, of perception.

You can choose not to act, yet I do not understand why people complicate themselves with barriers of meaning. Let us summarize life: to act, not to act, and to perceive. That is everything. Holding this perspective, I cannot comprehend the neurosis of people, when this summary makes so clear what the purpose of life is, and how it carries us until the end, surfing each situation and perception until one day our body stops.

The studies of Dr. Christopher S. Hyatt and his exercises on the vindication of the mind explain that the mind is a sort of erroneous association, a confusion machine, which goes, in essence, against its own most natural nature, in a neurotic and possessive manner. To be a radical logical perceptualist is to take life as it is, from beginning to end. If this seems boring to you, it is because it is boring, but I intend to eliminate certain levels of epistemological neurosis. I hope, however, that you know the cause of neurosis is going against the reality that happens to us, and that ceasing that contrary desire or suffering is perhaps the very reason it also ceases (Buddha).

I also hope you can distinguish between reality, which is a perhaps yes or a perhaps no. There is never an opposite. By surfing between the fact that reality is neither good nor bad, but rather any reality that allows us to become masters of opposites, we can rise toward a kind of conjunction where we are pure matter in unison with all universal laws, without distinguishing here or there. Only in this way do we become masters of totality, absolute totality, because by covering the complete range of experience, we become beings capable of excelling beyond absolute experience and living excellent lives. Make yourself master of every opposite, and you will never find yourself blocked before a limitation. As I said, it is to be master of the totality of life, formless and limitless, able to be everything or nothing, until the very end.
