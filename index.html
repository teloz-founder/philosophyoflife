⏤ uprooting · pure experience

Science tells us that human beings are organisms composed of cells. The mind, however, is a subjective overlay—an addition that often contradicts what is considered real and optimal. It surpasses mental constructs and even the world itself, pointing instead to a deeper nature: that of living beings who simply experience life and everything it contains, without further justification.

There are many methods to approach this, but in my case, it is more of an attitude of uprooting. It is the stance I once summarized as, “I do this because I feel like it,” or, more pragmatically, a state of absolute unconcern and total arrogance. This allows you to function in certain ways—though this is not the most perfect explanation, nor do I claim it to be optimal. I merely present it as one possible approach.

“ In any case, I created this space with the essence of that philosophy, so that others might enhance their experience of life in its rawest, purest form. ”

That, in short, is the core of a life that holds structures of meaning and continues to function until the very end.
