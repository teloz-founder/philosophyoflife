The essence of this philosophy is the thesis that human beings move against a merely primary nature of existence itself. That life is a constant flow, which, far from habitual forms and imposed limits, is what it is.

If this does not seem enough to you, let yourself be carried, and you will ascend to the true nature of reality — a more profoundly beautiful and authentic one.

Now allow yourself to be thus, and you will discover life as God brought you into the world.

Rest in this reality, until the end. A smile :)
This is not a system. It is an opening.
The goal is not to quiet the mind, but to keep it open to what comes.
